North East & Aberdeen – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Historically oil & gas–focused region now pivoting to energy transition, ports and innovation.
  • Significant port, harbour and logistics activity tied to offshore wind and energy supply chains.
  • Continuing investment in roads, public transport and regeneration to support diversification.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Energy Transition: enabling infrastructure for renewables, hydrogen and low-carbon hubs.
  • Ports & Harbours: quays, logistics, O&M bases and associated infrastructure.
  • Strategic Roads & Transport: connectivity to ports, industrial clusters and communities.
  • City & Town Regeneration: diversification away from oil & gas into mixed-use and innovation districts.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Port and harbour authorities.
  • Energy companies and developers (traditional and renewables).
  • Local authorities and regional economic partnerships.
  • Universities and research/innovation campuses.

How to Grow Gleeds in the North East & Aberdeen

  • Lead with energy transition capability – demonstrate experience from other UK regions and sectors.
  • Offer integrated Cost, PM and Controls for complex port and energy infrastructure portfolios.
  • Provide advisory support on funding, delivery models and risk allocation for new energy and regeneration projects.
  • Develop strategic relationships with anchor energy and port clients via targeted pilots and reviews.
  • Partner with local specialists to strengthen bids and reflect local delivery knowledge.

Next 12-Month Actions
