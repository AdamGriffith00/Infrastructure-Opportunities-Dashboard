County Durham & Darlington – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Mixed urban–rural area with transport, regeneration, energy and estate needs.
  • Opportunities around town and city-centre renewal, business parks, energy and connectivity.
  • Ongoing investment in housing, education and public buildings.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Transport: road, rail and active travel improvements linking towns, cities and employment sites.
  • Regeneration: town and city-centre programmes, mixed-use and business district developments.
  • Energy & Industrial: industrial estates, energy projects and enabling infrastructure.
  • Public Estate: schools, colleges, health and civic buildings.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • County and local councils, combined authority partners.
  • Transport and rail bodies.
  • Industrial and energy developers.
  • Education and health organisations.

How to Grow Gleeds in County Durham & Darlington

  • Target regeneration and growth programmes where we can offer full Cost + PM + Controls + Advisory support.
  • Provide PMO and governance support for multi-scheme portfolios (transport, regeneration, estate).
  • Lead on business cases and funding bids for key projects needing robust justification.
  • Offer embedded roles into local authorities and partners, supported by wider Gleeds expertise.
  • Leverage case studies from other UK regions to demonstrate delivery confidence.
