Tees Valley – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Industrial and port-focused area with significant freeport, energy transition and regeneration activity.
  • Large-scale projects around industrial decarbonisation, logistics, enabling works and access.
  • Ongoing need for transport connectivity, town-centre renewal and public realm.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Freeport & Ports: quays, logistics, O&M bases, utilities and enabling infrastructure.
  • Energy & Industrial: energy transition, industrial estates, grid connections and supporting works.
  • Transport: road, rail and active travel improvements linked to industrial and urban areas.
  • Urban Regeneration: town centres, housing and commercial-led schemes.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Combined authority and local councils.
  • Freeport and port authorities.
  • Industrial and energy developers.
  • Transport agencies and delivery partners.
