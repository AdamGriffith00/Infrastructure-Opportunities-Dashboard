North West & Causeway Coast – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Region built around Derry/Londonderry and the Causeway Coast with strong tourism, cross-border and city deal drivers.
  • Need for improved transport connectivity, public realm and regeneration in key towns and the city.
  • Tourism hotspots requiring visitor, coastal and environmental infrastructure.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • City & Town Regeneration: public realm, streets, waterfronts and mixed-use schemes.
  • Transport & Connectivity: roads, public transport and active travel improvements.
  • Tourism & Coastal: visitor infrastructure, coastal protection and amenity projects.
  • Public Estate: local health, education and civic buildings programmes.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • City and coastal councils and city deal partnership.
  • Transport and roads agencies.
  • Tourism and heritage bodies.
  • Health and education estate owners.

How to Grow Gleeds in the North West & Causeway Coast

  • Align with city deal and regeneration programmes and offer full Cost + PM + Controls + Advisory support.
  • Emphasise coastal, tourism and heritage-sensitive delivery in our case studies and approach.
  • Provide programme-level PMO and reporting for regeneration and transport portfolios.
  • Offer targeted advisory support for funding bids, economic cases and phasing strategies.
  • Partner with local designers and consultants to build strong, place-based bid teams.
