Glasgow & Clyde – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Scotland's largest urban area with heavy transport, regeneration and public estate demand.
  • Significant activity around city and growth deals, waterfront regeneration and low-carbon districts.
  • Ongoing need for rail, metro and bus improvements, plus road capacity and resilience schemes.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Urban Transport: rail, subway/metro, bus corridors, active travel and stations.
  • City & Growth Deal Projects: innovation districts, campuses and regeneration sites.
  • Public Estate: health, education and civic buildings looking to decarbonise and modernise.
  • Water & Drainage: resilience and surface water management in dense urban areas.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • City council and neighbouring authorities in the Clyde area.
  • Transport bodies and rail/metro operators.
  • Health boards, universities and colleges.
  • Developers and regeneration agencies on waterfront and inner-city sites.

How to Grow Gleeds in Glasgow & Clyde

  • Align to 2–3 flagship programmes (transport, regeneration, estates) where we can embed Cost, PM and Controls teams.
  • Offer portfolio-level PMO services for complex multi-funder city deal and regeneration portfolios.
  • Lead early-stage advisory and business case work for new transport corridors and regeneration schemes.
  • Deploy seconded staff into client teams to build trust and insight, backed by central technical support.
  • Showcase cross-UK experience in major city-region programmes and adapt lessons for Glasgow.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Map all active city/growth deal, transport and public estate programmes and identify decision-makers.
  • Schedule capability briefings with at least three key institutions (e.g. council, health board, university).
  • Target one major transport and one regeneration scheme for a full-service bid (Cost + PM + Controls + Advisory).
  • Capture Glasgow & Clyde benchmarks for urban infra costs, risk and delivery models.
