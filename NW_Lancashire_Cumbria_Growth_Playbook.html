Lancashire & Cumbria – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Mix of strategic roads, rail, energy and environmental schemes serving both rural and industrial communities.
  • Key role in national energy and grid infrastructure, plus flood risk and coastal protection.
  • Tourism destinations requiring sensitive, sustainable infrastructure upgrades.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Strategic & Major Roads: trunk road upgrades, junction improvements, safety and resilience schemes.
  • Energy & Grid: substations, reinforcements, connections and energy-cluster infrastructure.
  • Flood & Coastal: flood defences, coastal protection and river schemes.
  • Tourism & Place: town centres, waterfronts and visitor infrastructure improvements.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • National roads and rail bodies with regional schemes.
  • Utilities and energy companies (electricity, water, gas) with assets in the region.
  • County and unitary authorities across Lancashire and Cumbria.
  • Environment and flood authorities plus coastal partnerships.

How to Grow Gleeds in Lancashire & Cumbria

  • Focus on long-term programme roles (frameworks, alliances, PMO) rather than isolated one-off schemes.
  • Highlight our experience on complex, rural and environmentally sensitive projects, including access and logistics challenges.
  • Offer integrated cost, schedule and risk management for multi-year infrastructure portfolios.
  • Bring structured value and risk workshops early in project lifecycles to help clients prioritise limited budgets.
  • Partner with local consultants and contractors who bring local delivery understanding.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Compile a heatmap of upcoming transport, energy and flood schemes in the region using public sources.
  • Engage at least three key utilities/energy clients with a regional Gleeds briefing session.
  • Identify one strategic roads and one energy/grid opportunity to pursue with a multi-service bid team.
  • Build a case study pack of similar projects delivered elsewhere to use in bids and meetings.
