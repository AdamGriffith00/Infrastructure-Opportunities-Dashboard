Derbyshire – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Derby is a national hub for aerospace, rail and advanced manufacturing; wider county includes Peak District visitor economy.
  • Strategic growth along A50/A38 with logistics and supplier parks; town-centre regeneration across market towns.
  • Clean growth and industrial decarbonisation driving plant upgrades and energy projects.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Aerospace, Rail & Advanced Manufacturing: test cells, MRO, factory upgrades.
  • Supplier Parks & Logistics: modern units, utilities and access/enabling.
  • Town-Centre & Brownfield Regeneration: mixed-use, remediation, public realm.
  • Energy & Net Zero: industrial heat/power, grid connections, on-site renewables.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Derby City Council; Derbyshire County & districts; East Midlands Combined County Authority.
  • OEMs and Tier suppliers (aero/rail), innovation campuses, FE/HE.
  • Industrial estate owners and logistics developers along A50/A38.
  • Utilities, DNOs and energy developers.

How to Grow Gleeds in Derbyshire

  • Lead multi-plot enabling works for supplier parks with robust risk management.
  • Package town-centre renewals into framework-compatible lots with funding cases.
  • Provide decarb pathways for high-energy manufacturing estates.
