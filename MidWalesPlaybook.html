Mid Wales – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Predominantly rural economy with strengths in agri-food, forestry/timber, advanced materials SMEs and tourism.
  • Priority needs in digital & mobile connectivity, transport resilience and town-centre revitalisation.
  • Strong opportunities in renewables, storage and low-carbon heat for dispersed communities and estates.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Rural Digital & Utilities: fibre/5G infill, substation and network upgrades.
  • Agri-Tech & Food: processing, R&D pilot lines, cold chain and logistics hubs.
  • Town-Centre & Heritage Regeneration; housing-led mixed use.
  • Renewables & Heat: solar, wind, storage and district/rural heat solutions.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Local authorities and regional growth partnerships.
  • Utilities and digital network operators.
  • FE/HE and research centres; agri-food producers and co-ops.
  • NHS/public estate owners and housing associations.

How to Grow Gleeds in Mid Wales

  • Provide programme management for multi-settlement digital and utilities rollouts.
  • Lead funding cases for town-centre/heritage schemes and rural housing.
  • Deploy agri-tech delivery playbooks for processing and cold-chain projects.
  • Offer estate decarb audits and phased delivery plans for public assets.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Build a map of rural digital “not-spots” and prioritise three pilot communities.
  • Publish cost benchmarks for small-footprint processing plants and rural logistics hubs.
  • Secure one multi-town regeneration programme (framework-compatible lots).
