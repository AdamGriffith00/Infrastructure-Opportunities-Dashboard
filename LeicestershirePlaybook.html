Leicestershire – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Home to the UK’s logistics “golden triangle” (M1/M6/M69) and East Midlands Airport air cargo hub.
  • Growth in space/data, advanced manufacturing and R&D estates anchored by university assets.
  • Ongoing city and town regeneration with demand for sustainable offices and labs.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Space & Data/AI: research facilities, integration labs, and scale-up space.
  • Industrial & Logistics: large-format sheds, last-mile, grid and access upgrades.
  • University & Innovation Estates: lab-ready buildings and grow-on space.
  • Urban Regeneration: mixed-use, housing-led, transport-linked plots.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Leicester City; Leicestershire County & districts; East Midlands Airport stakeholders.
  • University and innovation campuses; research partners.
  • Logistics/industrial developers and major landlords.
  • Utilities/DNOs for power and digital capacity.
