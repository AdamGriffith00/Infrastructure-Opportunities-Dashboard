Greater Manchester – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Combined authority with a strong pipeline in public transport, active travel, town-centre regeneration and net-zero estates.
  • Major focus on bus reform, Metrolink, local rail integration and improving connectivity between town centres.
  • Significant estate decarbonisation and public-building retrofit agenda across councils, NHS and education.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Transport: Metrolink enhancements, bus priority, active travel corridors, local rail and station upgrades.
  • Regeneration: town-centre and mixed-use schemes with complex funding stacks and multiple stakeholders.
  • Public Estate: schools, colleges, health and blue-light programmes requiring robust cost and controls.
  • Utilities & Energy: grid and connection-related works that interface with city-region growth plans.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Combined authority / transport body – strategic transport and regeneration sponsor.
  • Core local authorities (e.g. Manchester, Salford, Bolton, Wigan, Oldham, Rochdale, Stockport, Tameside, Trafford).
  • University and FE/HE estates, NHS Trusts and blue-light organisations.
  • Private developers and JV partners on town-centre and mixed-use sites.

How to Grow Gleeds in Greater Manchester

  • Anchor around 2–3 flagship programmes where we can provide integrated Cost, PM and Controls (e.g. transport corridor, estates or regeneration programme).
  • Lead with programme-level PMO and cost assurance capability to manage multi-fund, multi-stakeholder portfolios.
  • Package our advisory and business case skills to help clients shape and defend investment cases.
  • Offer secondments or embedded roles for cost, PM and controls professionals into combined authority and council teams.
  • Connect with major consultants and designers already active in the city-region to form pursuit partnerships.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Map all live and upcoming transport and regeneration programmes and identify where existing frameworks give us access.
  • Agree a small pursuit team (Cost, PM, Controls, Advisory) to target 3–5 specific opportunities.
  • Arrange capability briefings with combined authority and at least three core councils, focused on cost, PMO and business case support.
  • Capture and recycle local benchmarks for unit costs, programme delivery models and funding structures.
