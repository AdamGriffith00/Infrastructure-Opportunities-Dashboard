Cheshire & Warrington – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Strong industrial and energy cluster with chemicals, manufacturing and logistics assets.
  • Strategic location between major conurbations with heavy road and rail usage.
  • Ongoing demand for housing, employment land and commercial space.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Industrial & Energy Cluster: enabling infrastructure, utilities, grid upgrades and environmental improvements.
  • Strategic Roads & Junctions: capacity, safety and resilience projects on major corridors.
  • Employment Sites & Business Parks: site servicing, commercial buildings and logistics hubs.
  • Housing & Mixed-Use: infrastructure and enabling works for new communities.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Local authorities across Cheshire and Warrington.
  • Industrial and energy businesses, including chemical and manufacturing operators.
  • Utilities and energy network operators.
  • Developers of business parks, logistics hubs and mixed-use schemes.

How to Grow Gleeds in Cheshire & Warrington

  • Lead with cost and commercial expertise on complex industrial and energy schemes where risk and interfaces are high.
  • Position PM and Controls teams to run infrastructure and enabling works portfolios for both public and private clients.
  • Offer advisory support on investment cases and delivery models for strategic employment and housing sites.
  • Develop long-term relationships with anchor industrial clients by starting with targeted audits, reviews or pilot projects.
  • Leverage successes in nearby regions (e.g. GM/LCR) to demonstrate capability and bring cross-boundary thinking.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Identify 5–10 strategic industrial/energy and employment sites and map current advisors and contractors.
  • Hold joint meetings with local authorities and key private-sector clients to position Gleeds as a shared partner.
  • Target at least one framework or long-term services appointment that covers this geography.
  • Capture case studies for industrial and energy infrastructure to use in bids and pitch decks.
