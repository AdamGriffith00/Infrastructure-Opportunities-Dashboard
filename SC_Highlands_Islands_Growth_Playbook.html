Highlands, Islands & North – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Large, sparsely populated geography with rural transport, ports, energy and resilience needs.
  • Key role in renewables, grid connections and coastal infrastructure.
  • Tourism and community-focused projects requiring sensitive, sustainable development.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Transport & Connectivity: rural roads, ferries, ports, airfields and active travel.
  • Energy & Grid: substations, reinforcements and connections to renewables.
  • Coastal & Flood: coastal protection, harbours and flood schemes.
  • Community & Tourism: visitor infrastructure, community facilities and small estates programmes.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Local authorities and island councils.
  • Transport and ferry agencies, port/harbour authorities.
  • Utilities and energy developers.
  • Tourism bodies and community trusts.

How to Grow Gleeds in the Highlands, Islands & North

  • Highlight experience in remote, challenging and environmentally sensitive locations.
  • Offer flexible deployment models (hybrid working, periodic site presence) to manage geography efficiently.
  • Focus on integrated cost, schedule and risk management where access, logistics and climate add uncertainty.
  • Provide advisory support on prioritisation and value optimisation for limited public budgets.
  • Partner with local consultants and contractors to build acceptance and local knowledge.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Develop a pipeline view of transport, energy and coastal projects in the Highlands, Islands and northern regions.
  • Engage at least two key authorities or agencies with a focused Gleeds capability session.
  • Identify one transport and one energy/coastal project suitable for a multi-service pursuit.
  • Capture best practice and risk lessons from remote projects to use in proposals and delivery plans.
