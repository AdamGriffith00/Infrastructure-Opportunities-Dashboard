South East Wales (Cardiff Capital Region) – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Capital-city economy with strengths in financial & professional services, media/creative, cyber/data and compound semiconductors.
  • Significant metro and rail investment, city-centre and waterfront regeneration, and university-led R&D campuses.
  • Growing demand for low-carbon retrofit, district energy, and digital connectivity across the region.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Innovation & R&D: semiconductor, cyber/data centres, and lab-ready spaces.
  • Urban Regeneration: mixed-use, waterfront and city/core commercial.
  • Transport & Stations: metro upgrades, interchanges, active travel.
  • Public Estate Decarbonisation: heat networks, renewables, deep retrofit.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Cardiff Capital Region, Transport for Wales, local authorities.
  • Universities (Cardiff, USW), innovation campuses and science parks.
  • Developers/investors in city-centre and waterfront schemes.
  • NHS/public estate owners and utility providers.
