Scope of Spend Figures – Addressable Market (UK Highways)

  • National Highways (Strategic Road Network – SRN) is funded through multi‑year Road Investment Strategies (RIS). For the RIS3 era (2025–30) the emphasis is on operations, maintenance, renewals and targeted enhancements rather than large new corridors.
  • Devolved trunk roads in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are funded separately through Transport Scotland, Welsh Government and DfI Roads. Their multi‑year capital plans add a substantial parallel pipeline of renewals, safety and resilience schemes on non‑SRN strategic routes.
  • Local Authority networks remain the largest part of the physical network by length. Highways maintenance, structures, local safety schemes and small enhancements are funded via DfT grants, devolved budgets and council capital programmes, often delivered through frameworks such as SCAPE, CCS, Pagabo, NEPO, YORtender and local consortia.
  • City‑region and CRSTS programmes (e.g. Greater Manchester, West Midlands, West Yorkshire and other combined authorities) are delivering multi‑year packages of bus priority, active travel and corridor improvements with significant highway interfaces.
  • Digital roads, carbon and climate resilience cuts across all of the above: ITS, signals, control centres, EV infrastructure, data and monitoring, plus flood/landslide and coastal resilience schemes embedded into renewals and upgrades.
  • This sheet aims to give a whole‑system UK highways view of the addressable market in and around 2026 (SRN, devolved trunk roads, Local Authority networks and city‑region corridors), using indicative ranges where individual programme allocations are not yet fixed.

This table brings together the main UK highways funding streams into an indicative one‑year picture for 2026. Figures are order‑of‑magnitude ranges for planning and positioning – they are not a forecast and will move with future Spending Reviews and local decisions.

Strategic Road Network – National Highways (RIS3) c.£6–7bn Annualised view of RIS‑era operations, maintenance, renewals and targeted enhancements on the SRN in England. Includes pavement, structures, safety, renewals and selected major projects.
Devolved trunk roads (Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland) c.£1.0–1.5bn Multi‑year capital programmes for Transport Scotland, Welsh Government / Trunk Road Agents and DfI Roads – covering renewals, safety, resilience and selected improvements.
