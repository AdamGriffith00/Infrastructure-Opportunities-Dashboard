South & Border Counties – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Mixed rural and urban area with strong cross-border, transport, regeneration and community dimensions.
  • Opportunities around transport corridors, town regeneration, energy and public estate.
  • Need to balance budget constraints with visible improvements to communities and services.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Transport: key roads, cross-border routes and active travel links.
  • Regeneration: town centres, mixed-use and public realm schemes.
  • Energy & Industrial: smaller industrial sites, renewables and enabling works.
  • Public Estate: schools, health and civic buildings, community assets.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Southern councils and cross-border partnerships.
  • Transport and infrastructure agencies.
  • Industrial, energy and logistics developers.
  • Health, education and community estate owners.

How to Grow Gleeds in the South & Border Counties

  • Target multi-scheme programmes (transport, regeneration, estates) rather than isolated projects.
  • Offer clear, structured advisory support for funding bids and cross-border initiatives.
  • Provide integrated Cost, PM and Controls to support delivery under tight budget constraints.
  • Offer embedded or part-time roles to councils and agencies, backed by regional expertise.
  • Use lessons learned from similar cross-border and rural regions across the UK & Ireland in our approach.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Compile a pipeline of transport, regeneration, energy and estate projects across southern and border councils.
  • Hold introductions and capability sessions with key council, agency and partnership stakeholders.
  • Identify one transport and one regeneration/estate programme for focused multi-service pursuit.
  • Capture a set of cross-border and rural case studies to support positioning and bid responses.
