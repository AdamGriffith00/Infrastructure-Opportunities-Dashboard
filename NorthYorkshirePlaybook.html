North Yorkshire – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Diverse rural–coastal–urban economy with strengths in bioeconomy, agri-food, tourism and science & tech anchored by York.
  • New York & North Yorkshire Combined Authority (YNYCA) forming investment pipelines and a Local Growth Plan.
  • Growing emphasis on low-carbon, digital and rural innovation infrastructure to boost productivity and inclusion.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Bioeconomy & Agri-Tech: research, pilot manufacturing, clean growth parks.
  • Rural Digital: 5G/fibre infill, data hubs, and smart-farming tech facilities.
  • Tourism & Heritage: visitor economy, active travel and cultural regeneration.
  • Education & Research Estates: university and innovation campus investment.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • York & North Yorkshire Combined Authority and constituent councils.
  • University of York and BioYorkshire partners.
  • Rural broadband and utilities operators.
  • Private developers in heritage, tourism and agri-tech sectors.

How to Grow Gleeds in North Yorkshire

  • Position on YNYCA Local Growth Plan projects—especially bioeconomy, net-zero and digital infrastructure schemes.
  • Showcase rural regeneration and university-linked delivery expertise to secure advisory roles.
  • Target innovation and skills capital projects (labs, training centres, digital hubs) where structured PM and cost assurance are key.
  • Leverage Levelling Up, Shared Prosperity and Rural England funds by providing business-case support.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Engage YNYCA’s investment team to identify early-stage pipeline schemes for advisory input.
  • Develop cost benchmarks for agri-tech pilot and rural digital projects.
  • Target one BioYorkshire innovation or skills campus for a full Cost + PM + Controls offer.
  • Build relationships with rural broadband and renewable developers for framework inclusion.
