Humber – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • UK’s leading offshore wind and green port cluster, with major O&M bases at Hull and Grimsby.
  • Growing opportunities in hydrogen, ammonia and carbon-capture logistics through the “Energy Estuary”.
  • Significant urban regeneration and industrial-estate reuse in Hull and North Lincolnshire.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Offshore Wind O&M and Port Infrastructure.
  • Hydrogen, Carbon Capture and Green Fuels facilities.
  • Composites and Circular Manufacturing (blade repair/recycling).
  • Urban Regeneration and Skills Hubs linked to net-zero transition.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Hull City Council, East Riding, and North & North East Lincolnshire councils.
  • Major port operators (ABP, Ørsted, Siemens Gamesa, RWE).
  • Humber Freeport and regional business boards.
  • Industrial and logistics developers around the estuary.

How to Grow Gleeds in Humber

  • Target offshore wind, hydrogen and port-related capital projects requiring integrated cost and PM expertise.
  • Position on Freeport enabling works (access, utilities, site prep) with full risk and controls delivery.
  • Develop circular manufacturing and retrofit cost intelligence for sustainability funding bids.
  • Support local skills and training infrastructure through advisory and project-setup services.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Engage Humber Freeport and local authorities to identify investible port and energy projects.
  • Produce cost models for offshore O&M facilities, hydrogen hubs and composite recycling sites.
  • Develop partnerships with OEMs for framework tendering on expansion projects.
  • Target one flagship port regeneration or green-fuels hub for full Gleeds delivery offer.
