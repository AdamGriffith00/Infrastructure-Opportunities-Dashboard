Black Country – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Historic heart of UK manufacturing with metals, fabrication, casting, automotive supply chain and logistics.
  • Significant brownfield regeneration and estate renewal across Dudley, Sandwell, Walsall and Wolverhampton.
  • Transport connectivity improvements and town-centre revival programmes driving demand.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Industrial & Logistics: modern sheds, advanced manufacturing units, low-carbon retrofits.
  • Brownfield & Housing-Led Regeneration: remediation, enabling works, mixed-use.
  • Skills & Further Education Estates: technical training centres aligned to manufacturing 4.0.
  • Local Transport & Public Realm: corridors, interchanges, active travel.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Black Country local authorities and WMCA.
  • Homes England; development corporations and urban regeneration companies.
  • Industrial estate owners, logistics developers and SME manufacturers.
  • FE colleges and training providers; NHS/public estate owners.

How to Grow Gleeds in the Black Country

  • Lead brownfield enablement (ground, utilities, access) with robust cost, risk and programme controls.
  • Offer scalable frameworks for multi-plot industrial/SME refurb and net-zero upgrades.
  • Shape bids and business cases for housing, town-centre and skills capital funding.
  • Deploy regional benchmarks to de-risk budget setting and procurement.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Create a pipeline register of brownfield/town-centre projects and enabling packages.
  • Publish cost models for remediation, industrial retrofit and small-unit new build.
  • Target one multi-plot industrial estate upgrade for programme-wide PM/Controls.
  • Set up a quarterly developer roundtable to surface early opportunities.
