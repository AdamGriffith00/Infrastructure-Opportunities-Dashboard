South Yorkshire – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Strong advanced manufacturing, clean energy and digital economy centred on Sheffield, Rotherham, Doncaster and Barnsley.
  • Anchored by the Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre (AMRC) and University of Sheffield assets.
  • Significant regeneration and airport reinstatement programmes shaping 2025–2030 investment.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Advanced Manufacturing & Defence Supply Chain facilities.
  • Clean Energy & Sustainable Aviation infrastructure.
  • Digital and Creative Tech campuses.
  • Urban Regeneration around Sheffield and Doncaster growth corridors.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority and local councils.
  • University of Sheffield / AMRC network and Catapult partners.
  • Private aerospace, defence and renewables manufacturers.
  • Development corporations and airport regeneration bodies.

How to Grow Gleeds in South Yorkshire

  • Embed within AMRC supplier and SME innovation programmes to provide PM and cost support for factory upgrades.
  • Target aviation, logistics and clean-energy clusters tied to the airport and hydrogen programmes.
  • Develop a Defence & Advanced Manufacturing framework offer highlighting cost control and risk management expertise.
  • Leverage local university and Catapult partnerships for early business-case and funding advisory roles.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Engage SYMCA and AMRC to identify pipeline industrial sites and shared R&D facilities.
  • Map and target three clean-energy or sustainable aviation schemes for tender readiness.
  • Produce cost data for advanced manufacturing retrofits and low-carbon factory builds.
  • Establish links with defence and space OEM supply-chain programmes for project controls support.
