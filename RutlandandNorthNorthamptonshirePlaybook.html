Rutland & North Northamptonshire – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Predominantly rural Rutland with tourism and high-quality living; North Northamptonshire includes Corby, Kettering, Wellingborough & East Northants.
  • Strategic logistics/manufacturing along the A14/M1/M6 corridors; town-centre renewal across market towns and growth areas.
  • Opportunities in advanced manufacturing, construction materials, food processing and estate decarbonisation.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Manufacturing & Materials: plant upgrades, clean production, testing labs.
  • Town-Centre & Housing-Led Regeneration: remediation, enabling and public realm.
  • Energy & Digital: grid upgrades, renewables and rural connectivity infill.
