North & East Corridor – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Coastal and commuter corridor including key towns and industrial/logistics sites.
  • Important area for roads, rail, ports, logistics and housing growth.
  • Demand for public realm, active travel and place-making in towns and coastal communities.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Transport & Connectivity: trunk and local roads, rail, park-and-ride and active travel.
  • Ports & Logistics: port-related infrastructure, logistics hubs and industrial sites.
  • Housing & Mixed-Use: enabling infrastructure and public realm for new communities.
  • Public Estate & Education: school and college projects, community and civic buildings.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Coastal and corridor councils.
  • Transport and port authorities.
  • Developers and housing associations.
  • Education and public estate bodies.

How to Grow Gleeds in the North & East Corridor

  • Focus on corridor-wide and town-centre programmes where we can provide Cost + PM + Controls at portfolio level.
  • Lead on business cases and funding strategies for transport and regeneration schemes.
  • Emphasise our experience with ports, logistics and housing enabling infrastructure.
  • Offer embedded roles into key council and agency teams with back-up from regional experts.
  • Develop relationships with developers and housing associations around repeat infrastructure programmes.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Arrange meetings with at least two key councils plus transport/port stakeholders.
  • Identify one transport corridor and one housing/mixed-use opportunity for multi-service pursuit.
