Belfast City Region – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Capital city region with the largest concentration of transport, regeneration and public estate projects in NI.
  • City deal and regeneration programmes driving innovation, university, cultural and waterfront schemes.
  • Strong demand for public realm, transport corridors, campus and health estate investment.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Transport & Connectivity: bus and rail corridors, active travel and city-centre access schemes.
  • City & Growth Deal Projects: innovation, university and cultural projects, waterfront and regeneration.
  • Public Estate: health, education, justice and central government buildings.
  • Utilities & Enabling Works: utilities upgrades, site servicing and enabling infrastructure.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • City council and surrounding councils in the city region.
  • Transport and infrastructure departments/agencies.
  • Universities, colleges and health trusts.
  • Developers and regeneration agencies on major city sites.

How to Grow Gleeds in Belfast City Region

  • Anchor around 2–3 flagship city deal or regeneration programmes with integrated Cost + PM + Controls teams.
  • Offer portfolio-level PMO and reporting for city deal, estates and transport programmes.
  • Lead early-stage advisory and business case support for major city-region schemes and funding bids.
  • Provide embedded roles / secondments into city deal, council and institutional client teams.
  • Use digital dashboards to provide clear, portfolio-wide views for senior officials and funders.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Map all active city deal, regeneration and transport programmes and identify senior sponsors.
  • Hold capability sessions with key city-region stakeholders (council, transport, university, health).
  • Target one major city deal project and one estate programme for multi-service pursuit.
  • Capture Belfast-focused benchmarks on costs, delivery models and programme governance.
