North Wales – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Industrial and coastal economy with strengths in advanced manufacturing (Dee corridor), renewables/offshore, and tourism.
  • Strategic energy opportunities including nuclear/new energy potential and grid/port upgrades.
  • Connectivity focus on border logistics, rail improvements and town-centre regeneration across key settlements.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Industrial & Advanced Manufacturing campuses (clean rooms, flexible units).
  • Energy & Nuclear-readiness (port/land enabling, skills & logistics hubs).
  • Ports & Coastal Infrastructure (O&M, storage, laydown, resilience).
  • Regeneration & Housing (brownfield enablement, town-centre renewal).

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • North Wales Economic Ambition Board and local authorities.
  • Port operators and energy developers; grid and utilities.
  • OEMs/Tier suppliers in the Dee/Holyhead corridors.
  • FE/HE providers and NHS/public estate owners.

How to Grow Gleeds in North Wales

  • Lead enabling works and programme controls for energy and port-linked projects.
  • Provide capex assurance on industrial/clean-room builds and retrofits.
  • Package multi-town regeneration into framework-friendly lots.
  • Offer skills/estates business cases aligned to prospective energy growth.
