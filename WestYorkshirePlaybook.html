West Yorkshire – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • UK’s second financial centre outside London with strong digital, fintech, legal and health-tech clusters.
  • Investment Zone designation for HealthTech & Digital industries driving R&D property demand.
  • Major transport and regeneration investments planned through the West Yorkshire Combined Authority (WYCA).

Priority Sectors for Us

  • HealthTech & Digital R&D facilities.
  • FinTech, LegalTech and corporate relocation projects.
  • Urban Regeneration and city-centre mixed-use development.
  • Transport infrastructure and mass-transit enabling works.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • West Yorkshire Combined Authority and constituent councils.
  • Universities of Leeds, Bradford and Huddersfield.
  • BoE Northern Hub, NHS and digital innovation partners.
  • Developers, IZ consortia and investors in tech and regeneration schemes.

  • Target financial, legal and tech relocations seeking sustainable office and lab space solutions.
  • Strengthen regeneration credentials through early business-case and delivery-assurance roles with WYCA.
  • Integrate transport and property advisory to serve cross-sector regeneration corridors.
