Scope of Spend Figures – Addressable Market (UK Utilities)

  • Water & wastewater – AMP8 (2025–2030) provides a material step-up in investment for water quality, storm overflows, resilience and growth across the English & Welsh water companies. Scottish Water and NI Water have parallel capital programmes with their own regulatory cycles.
  • Electricity networks – RIIO-ED2 (DNOs) and RIIO-T2/T3 (transmission) underpin multi-year portfolios for connections, capacity growth, asset health and flexibility services to enable net zero.
  • Gas networks continue to invest in safety, iron mains replacement, pressure management and early hydrogen / low‑carbon heat trials, with future price controls likely to reshape the mix.
  • Cross-cutting energy transition infrastructure – including large-scale renewables grid connections, storage, EV charging infrastructure, hydrogen and district heating interfaces – cuts across multiple utilities and often involves joint ventures with developers and local authorities.
  • Flood & coastal / resilience interfaces – Environment Agency and local authority programmes (often co-funded with utilities) are increasingly linked to treatment works, networks and substations to protect critical assets from climate risk.
  • This sheet aims to give a whole-system UK utilities view of the addressable market around 2026, blending water, wastewater, electricity, gas and related resilience/energy-transition investment. Figures are indicative and should be refreshed as PR24/AMP8, RIIO and local funding decisions evolve.
