Scope of Spend Figures – Addressable Market (UK Rail)

  • CP7 England & Wales – £44bn (2024–29) covers operations, maintenance and renewals on the Network Rail mainline, excluding enhancements. This is roughly £8–9bn per year of addressable infrastructure spend.
  • Scotland – £4.8bn (2024–29) is a separate settlement for Scotland's Railway, adding approximately £0.9–1.0bn per year of renewals and maintenance investment to the GB total.
  • Enhancements & Integrated Rail Plan (IRP – c.£96bn) sit on top of CP7. 2026 will see design and construction activity across schemes such as Transpennine Route Upgrade, Midland Main Line electrification, East Coast corridor upgrades and East West Rail. Funding is released project‑by‑project rather than via a single central pot.
  • Devolved metro / light rail systems (TfL: LU, London Overground, DLR; Tyne & Wear Metro; Metrolink; West Midlands Metro; South Wales Metro; Glasgow Subway and others) operate under their own multi‑year capital plans and City Region Sustainable Transport Settlements. These are included in this addressable view as part of the wider UK rail system.
  • HS2 and other major projects continue as a multi‑billion‑pound annual programme through the late 2020s, covering high‑speed civil works, stations, interfaces with the classic network and regeneration around key hubs (e.g. Euston).
  • Northern Ireland & rolling stock / depots – Translink/DfI rail renewals, depots and cross‑border works, together with UK rolling stock and depot investment, are recognised as part of the overall rail market even where procurement routes differ from core Network Rail spend.
  • This sheet therefore aims to give a whole‑system UK rail view of the addressable market in and around 2026 (mainline, metro, high‑speed, light rail and depots), using indicative ranges where formal 2026 allocations are not yet fixed.
