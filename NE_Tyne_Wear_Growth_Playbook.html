Tyne & Wear – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Conurbation centred on Newcastle, Gateshead, Sunderland and surrounding areas with strong transport, urban regeneration and public estate needs.
  • Active programmes around metro and rail, bus and active travel, riverfront and city-centre regeneration.
  • Key role in connecting to wider regional manufacturing, ports and freeport assets.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Urban Transport: metro/rail, bus corridors, active travel and station improvements.
  • Regeneration: city and town centres, riverfront and brownfield redevelopment.
  • Public Estate: education, health, council and blue-light buildings.
  • Enabling Works: utilities, public realm and access infrastructure for new developments.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Combined/regional transport authority and metro/rail operators.
  • Core local authorities across Tyne & Wear.
  • Universities, colleges and health bodies.
  • Developers and regeneration agencies.

How to Grow Gleeds in Tyne & Wear

  • Target a small number of flagship programmes (metro/rail, urban corridors, city deals) for integrated Cost + PM + Controls roles.
  • Offer portfolio-level PMO support for transport and regeneration programmes with multiple schemes and funders.
  • Lead cost assurance and commercial challenge on complex capital programmes.
  • Offer embedded staff and secondments into key client teams backed by central Gleeds expertise.
  • Showcase digital dashboards to demonstrate how we can track performance across large portfolios.
