South West Wales (Swansea Bay City Region) – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Port and university region with strengths in marine energy, advanced manufacturing, digital and health innovation.
  • Significant waterfront and city-centre regeneration in Swansea and coastal towns.
  • Opportunities in renewables, hydrogen and industrial decarbonisation across coastal assets and estates.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Marine & Port Infrastructure; offshore O&M bases and energy hubs.
  • University & Innovation Estates; health-tech and digital campuses.
  • Urban Regeneration; mixed-use, housing-led and cultural/public realm.
  • Energy & Industrial Decarb; hydrogen-readiness and heat networks.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Local authorities across the city region; Swansea University and UWTSD.
  • Port operators and energy developers.
  • NHS and public estate owners.
  • Private developers and JV partners for waterfront schemes.

How to Grow Gleeds in Swansea Bay

  • Target coastal energy and port-linked projects requiring robust programme controls.
  • Lead business cases for university health/digital facilities and town-centre renewal.
  • Offer end-to-end enabling works management (ground, utilities, access) for complex waterfront plots.
  • Deploy decarb audits to unlock funding for public estate retrofit and heat networks.
