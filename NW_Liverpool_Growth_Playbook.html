Liverpool City Region – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Port-focused region with logistics, maritime, freeport and waterfront regeneration at its core.
  • Ongoing investment in local rail, bus and active travel to connect communities to jobs and the port.
  • Large regeneration potential around waterfronts, town centres and brownfield sites.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Ports & Logistics: quays, warehousing, distribution hubs, access roads and rail connections.
  • Freeport-linked: sites and enabling infrastructure around the freeport tax and customs zones.
  • Urban Regeneration: housing-led schemes, town-centre and waterfront projects.
  • Local Transport: rail upgrades, stations, bus corridors and active travel links.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • Combined authority and transport body – commissioners of major transport and regeneration programmes.
  • Port operator(s) and logistics developers.
  • Local authorities across the city region.
  • Private developers and JV partners on waterfront and regeneration schemes.

How to Grow Gleeds in Liverpool City Region

  • Target port and freeport-related infrastructure where cost, PM and risk/controls are critical for complex multi-party schemes.
  • Position our advisory team on business cases and funding packages (e.g. Levelling Up, transport funds, freeport investment).
  • Demonstrate port, marine and logistics experience from elsewhere and adapt lessons learned to local needs.
  • Offer integrated support across enabling works (utilities, ground, access), not just core vertical builds.
  • Collaborate with established local architects and engineers to front strong multi-disciplinary bids.
