Northumberland & Borders – Infrastructure Growth Playbook

One-pager for Cost Management, Project Management, Project Controls and Advisory

Market Snapshot

  • Predominantly rural area with key transport, energy, tourism and community infrastructure needs.
  • Important route for north–south connectivity and cross-border movements.
  • Demand for resilience, safety and environmental improvements to roads, coastal and community assets.

Priority Sectors for Us

  • Roads & Connectivity: trunk and local roads, junctions and safety schemes.
  • Energy & Grid: substations, reinforcements and connections to renewables and communities.
  • Coastal & Flood: coastal protection, flood defences and river schemes.
  • Tourism & Community: visitor infrastructure, public realm and local facilities.

Key Clients & Stakeholders

  • County council and neighbouring authorities.
  • National and regional roads bodies.
  • Utilities and energy developers.
  • Tourism bodies and community organisations.

How to Grow Gleeds in Northumberland & Borders

  • Highlight experience in rural, remote and environmentally sensitive projects.
  • Offer integrated cost, schedule and risk management to address logistics and access challenges.
  • Provide advisory support on prioritising limited budgets and optimising value for communities.
  • Develop partnerships with local consultants and contractors to strengthen proximity and acceptance.
  • Target long-term framework or programme roles rather than isolated single schemes.

Next 12-Month Actions

  • Develop a pipeline view of transport, energy and coastal projects across Northumberland and bordering areas.
  • Engage at least two key authorities or agencies with a regional Gleeds capability session.
  • Identify one transport and one energy/coastal project suitable for a multi-service pursuit.
  • Capture lessons from similar rural projects elsewhere to support proposals and delivery.
